Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 23, 2025
Last Updated: June 23, 2025

1. Who We Are

SkyLinx Global Solutions ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") operates the HRMS platform available at skylinxhrms.qzz (the "Platform"). We are a Business Process Outsourcing and IT Services company headquartered in India.

For any privacy-related concerns, contact us at: info@emplinx.com

2. What Information We Collect

When companies and their employees use our Platform, we collect the following types of data:

2.1 Information Provided by the Employer (Company Admin)

  • Company name, registration details, and billing information
  • Employee names, employee IDs, job titles, departments
  • Employment contracts and offer letters
  • Salary, payroll, and compensation data
  • Bank account details (for payroll processing)
  • Leave balances and attendance records

2.2 Information Provided Directly by Employees

  • Full name, date of birth, gender
  • Contact details (email address, phone number)
  • Residential address
  • Government-issued ID numbers (Aadhaar, PAN) where required by the employer
  • Emergency contact information
  • Profile photographs
  • Biometric/Face Data & Live Location: If your employer enables "Selfie Attendance" or geo-tracking, we may collect and process your facial image data and live GPS location solely for the purpose of verifying attendance. Face data is not shared with third parties.

2.3 Automatically Collected Data

  • Login timestamps and IP addresses
  • Browser type and device information
  • Session activity logs (pages visited, actions performed)
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 9)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide the HRMS service: Processing attendance, payroll, leave management, and related HR functions
  • Account management: Creating and maintaining user and company accounts
  • Security & fraud prevention: Monitoring for unauthorized access or suspicious activity
  • Legal compliance: Meeting obligations under Indian law (IT Act 2000, SPDI Rules 2011, Labour laws)
  • Customer support: Responding to queries and resolving issues
  • Service improvements: Analyzing usage patterns to improve Platform features
  • Communications: Sending promotional and transactional messages via SMS, WhatsApp, Email, or push notifications, even if your number is registered on the DND registry.

We do not use employee data for marketing purposes or sell it to third parties.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (India — SPDI Rules 2011)

As per the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, we classify and handle Sensitive Personal Data or Information (SPDI) — including financial data, biometric data, and government IDs — with explicit consent obtained at onboarding.

By using the Platform, both employers and employees acknowledge and consent to the data processing described in this Policy.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We may share data only in these limited situations:

Recipient Purpose
Oracle Cloud (Infrastructure) Secure data hosting and storage
Payment processors Processing subscription payments from employers
Analytics & Crash Reporting Tools Monitoring platform health and performance (e.g., Sentry, Firebase)
Government authorities When required by law or court order
Auditors/legal advisors For legal compliance purposes

All third-party service providers are contractually bound to maintain data confidentiality.

6. Data Storage & Security

  • All data is stored on Oracle Cloud servers located in India
  • We implement industry-standard security measures including:
  • AES-256 encryption at rest
  • TLS 1.2+ encryption in transit
  • Role-based access controls (RBAC)
  • Regular security audits
  • Automatic session timeouts
  • We maintain logs of all data access events

7. Data Retention

Data Type Retention Period
Employee personal data Duration of employment + 3 years
Payroll records 8 years (as per Indian statutory requirement)
Attendance records 3 years
Login/access logs 1 year
Account data after deletion 30 days, then permanently deleted

8. Your Rights

As a user (employee or employer), you have the right to:

  • Access — Request a copy of your personal data held by us
  • Correction — Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Withdrawal of Consent — Withdraw your consent to data processing at any time
  • Data Portability — Request your data in a machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@emplinx.com with the subject line: "Privacy Rights Request". We will respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies

Our Platform uses cookies to: - Maintain your login session - Remember your preferences - Analyze Platform usage (via internal analytics only)

You may disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect Platform functionality.

10. Children's Privacy

Our Platform is intended for use by businesses and adult professionals only. We do not knowingly collect data from individuals under the age of 18.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will: - Update the "Last Updated" date at the top - Notify Company Admins via email or in-app notification - Require re-acceptance if the changes are material

Continued use of the Platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
